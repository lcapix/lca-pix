# Walkthrough: LCAPIX for teaching and research (narration)

The narration of the walkthrough film, scene by scene, 16 October 2026.

### map · (scene 0, map)

LCAPIX for teaching and research. The new app, screen by screen.

Every screen sits on one canvas in Paper, in six rows, read from left to right.

We follow one professor, one sample study and one student.

## 1. Arrive

*From the website to her first Home*

One. Elena arrives.

### The landing page shows the product at work · (scene 2, map)

Dr. Elena Rivera arrives from the website. The headline: know the footprint of anything you make.

Below it, the product at work: a process tree for a painted steel bracket, and its answer, one point seven two.

A walkthrough card steps through the result, one view at a time.

### Every product, taken apart like a technical drawing · (scene 3, map)

Further down, the use cases: every product, taken apart like a technical drawing.

Six plates show what it models, from metal fabrication to automotive and energy.

### Sign-up: Continue with Google comes first · (scene 4, map)

She clicks Get started. Continue with Google is the first stop.

On the left, a blueprint of the bracket she will start on: sheet one of two.

When she comes back, she logs in. If something is wrong, the error shows on the line, under the field.

### About you: one step, and Guided mode stays off · (scene 5, map)

One setup step: her name, her institution if she likes, and what she will use it for.

A company workspace is in development. Guided mode is a beta, off unless she turns it on.

Sheet two of two traces the five tiers, from the product down to one task.

### Building the sample takes a few seconds · (scene 6, map)

She opens the sample study. It builds in about five seconds.

If the build fails, nothing is lost. She can try again, or start with an empty project.

## 2. Read the finished study

*Home is the sample, read in the order research is done*

Two. She reads a finished study.

### Home is a finished study, read in research order · (scene 8, map)

Her first Home is the finished sample study, read only, made for her when she signed up.

It reads in the order research is done. First, the question it asks.

Then how the bracket is made: seventeen steps in five tiers.

Then what goes in. Each impact is an amount times a factor, and every line can be checked.

### The answer, what builds it, and how sure · (scene 9, map)

The answer: one point seven two kilograms of CO2 equivalent per bracket.

Eighty-nine percent comes from cutting the blank, almost all of it steel. Powder coat and cure adds eight point one percent, the weld tab two point four.

How sure? Steel uses a global average, so the total is a sound estimate, not a precise one.

### A what-if, the write-up, and what next · (scene 10, map)

A what-if, a shorter cure cycle, gives one point six six seven. That is three point one percent lower.

An example write-up says what the numbers mean, in words.

And what next: she makes her own copy, to try it.

## 3. Try it on her own copy

*Tree, Inventory, Results and costs, on a copy she can change*

Three. She tries it on her own copy.

### The Tree tab: five lanes, product at the top · (scene 12, map)

Her copy opens on the Tree tab. It is named after her, and not run yet.

Five labelled lanes: the product at the top, the tasks at the bottom.

Two steps still need an amount. Until then, they are left out of a run.

### The step panel: each flow is an amount times a factor · (scene 13, map)

The floating panel shows the step's flows. Zero point eight kilograms of steel, times one point nine, makes one point five two.

The factor shows its source: a global average, because there is no US value.

Guided mode sets the exercise here. It is a beta.

### Hide the panel, and the canvas takes the full width · (scene 14, map)

Hide the panel, and the canvas takes the full width. Show panel brings it back.

### Keys, the step menu, and the state of each run · (scene 15, map)

Everything works from the keyboard: Tab, the arrows, Enter, and M for the step menu.

The step menu renames, moves or deletes a step.

A run in progress shows at the top. After a change, the run is marked out of date.

### Inventory: every flow, with its status · (scene 16, map)

Inventory lists every flow with its status: counted, no climate factor, needs an amount, or left out.

For deburring, the machine calculator takes zero point seven five kilowatts, eight seconds, at eighty percent load.

That is zero point zero zero one three kilowatt hours, put on the row in one click.

### Results: the answer, and the cost beside it · (scene 17, map)

Run one oh two gives one point seven two, the same as the base case.

Cost sits beside impact: one dollar seventy-five per bracket. The weld tab is thirty-one percent of the cost, but two point four percent of the impact.

The panel can dock to either side.

### Step costs: each one is a driver times a rate · (scene 18, map)

The Cost view shows each step's cost. Together they add up to one dollar seventy-five.

The weld tab is fifty-five cents. Four of its six activities are set, and each one is a driver times a rate.

The labour rate comes from BLS wages, or she enters her own.

## 4. Her own project, from a file

*A routing, a bill of materials, and a file that fails*

Four. Her own project, from a file.

### Start a project: the question comes first · (scene 20, map)

Now her own project: an aluminium enclosure. The question comes first, one unit for every case.

She starts from a file. Nothing is made until she checks it and saves.

### Add a file: a routing, and what it can give · (scene 21, map)

She adds a routing: the steps in order, and their hours. A PDF is read by AI.

Other file types are coming soon, like utility bills and cost sheets.

Each read counts. This one uses one of her ten reads this hour.

### Check what we read: five steps and their hours · (scene 22, map)

The reader finds five steps and their hours. Each one is ticked, for her to check.

Notes say what it left out, like setup time per lot.

Nothing is saved until she presses Save.

### Saved: where the case stands, and what comes next · (scene 23, map)

Saved. A checklist shows the tree and the labour hours done, and materials, energy and a run not yet.

Guided mode picks up from here, as a beta.

### The bill of materials: each line goes on a step · (scene 24, map)

Next, the bill of materials. Each line is matched to a material, with a score.

Every line needs a step before she can save. Three are left.

### When a file cannot be read · (scene 25, map)

If a file can't be read, like this scanned PDF, the fix is right there.

And reads have a limit: ten an hour. Here she waits eighteen minutes.

### Or start empty: the five lanes and the first step · (scene 26, map)

Or she starts empty: the same five lanes, with the product at the top.

The first step goes under the product. She picks its tier, and gives it a name.

## 5. Teach with it

*The class project: cases, people, compare, analytics and reports*

Five. She teaches with it.

### Home, later: her projects and what needs her · (scene 28, map)

Weeks later, Home is about her own work. Continue picks up where she stopped.

Needs attention lists what is waiting, like nine student cases with no run yet.

Her class project has thirty-four cases. The sample study steps aside.

### Many projects, and the sidebar stays calm · (scene 29, map)

A lab can have ten projects. The switcher keeps the sidebar calm.

### Cases: what exists in each student’s work · (scene 30, map)

Cases shows what exists in each student's work, as a pipeline from not run yet to hand-in.

No reminders go out, and grades stay in Canvas.

The same cases also show as a table.

### A new case is a what-if copy · (scene 31, map)

A new case is a what-if: copy the base case, and change one thing.

Steps and flows come along. Runs are not copied.

### People: add by email or a class list · (scene 32, map)

People are added by email or a class list. They need an account, and no email is sent.

Each person can change, or can only read.

One switch can show students only their own case.

Turning it on asks first, and names who it affects.

### Compare: fair only when the checks pass · (scene 33, map)

Compare puts two cases side by side: one point seven two, and one point six six seven for the shorter cure.

Four checks must pass first: method, region, unit, and current runs. Here they do.

When a check fails, it says why, and what to do.

### Analytics and reports: how far the class got · (scene 34, map)

Analytics: twenty-three of thirty-two cases ran, twelve wrote up, and five are ticked as hand-in.

Each dot is one run. They landed between one point six one and one point seven five.

Reports export a case as a PDF, a PowerPoint or a CSV. The PDF prints run three oh one as it ran.
